# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Travel Personality Analyzer is a small open-source project. Everyone who participates — whether they open an issue, review a pull request, or talk about the project in public — should be able to do that without harassment.

We pledge to make participation a respectful experience, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are learning.
- Disagree with the change, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may say no.
- Give feedback that someone else can act on.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, slurs, or sexual language aimed at another person.
- Public or private harassment, including unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a physical address, without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make someone leave the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems to Ryan Editraj at ryaneditraj@gmail.com. Include links or screenshots if you have them.

Ryan will review the report, reply when there is enough information, and may remove comments, close issues, or block further participation. Reports are kept private except where sharing them is required to handle the problem.

This project is maintained by one person. If a report is about the maintainer and you do not get a reply, you can contact GitHub through its abuse reporting tools.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in GitHub issues, pull requests, and any other space where someone is representing this project.
